## Thunderbirds
(Konami)
Thunderbirds Official Site

MOVIE: *Thunderbird 6*

### Outline 
Thunderbirds was established by the multi-millionaire Jeff Tracy as the International Rescue Organization (IR). Its base was constructed in secret on an island in the South Seas. While outwardly appearing to be the Tracy family's vacation home, a massive secret base lay concealed beneath it.
When a disaster struck, the team would detect it immediately—whether through direct radio requests for deployment or by intercepting signals via the space station Thunderbird 5. Jeff's five sons would then pilot their respective Thunderbird craft to the scene, swiftly carrying out rescue operations and disaster prevention measures.
Each machine featured a highly rational, automated design, allowing most of the rescue vehicles to be operated by a single person.
These machines are believed to have served as a model for the mecha used in later Japanese *tokusatsu* (special effects) productions.

### Thunderbird 1

An ultra-high-speed rocket craft that spearheads rescue operations. It launches from beneath the swimming pool at the secret base, ascending vertically using four rocket boosters before engaging its fan-jet engines in mid-air. During the transition from vertical launch to horizontal flight, a gyro-stabilization system keeps the cockpit level. It features variable-sweep wings that extend during low-speed flight and hovering. In addition to its main jet engine, it is equipped with VTOL (Vertical Take-Off and Landing) lift engines, enabling it to land on and take off from virtually any type of uneven terrain. As the International Rescue team's command and reconnaissance craft, it is the first to arrive at the disaster site; it analyzes the situation and coordinates the operations of Thunderbird 2. The craft is piloted using instrument readings and visual feeds projected onto screens; consequently, the cockpit windows remain closed during normal operation. While the landing gear consisted of wheels in the first episode, it was modified to skids from the second episode onwards to handle uneven terrain. It is equipped with a mobile command system that serves as the core of on-site operations. To maintain International Rescue's secrecy, it utilizes a nose-mounted cannon depending on the situation. The pilot is Scott.

### Thunderbird 2

A massive transport aircraft that serves as the hub of rescue operations. It deploys from beneath the terrace of the secret base and launches using a catapult. Although its flight speed is slower than the other craft, it rushes to disaster sites carrying various rescue vehicles in a large pod housed in the center of its fuselage. Operations are largely automated, though the pilot, Virgil, often personally operates the individual rescue vehicles during missions.


### Thunderbird 3

International Rescue's rocket for space-based rescue operations. It is primarily used for routine supply runs to Thunderbird 5. It launches from a pad located in the center of the secret base's circular structure. Powered by a nuclear reactor and propelled by chemical rockets, it is the only International Rescue craft capable of spaceflight. Navigation is controlled by a flight computer, and it is equipped with a docking control system for connecting with Thunderbird 5. The pilot is Alan. It plays a vital role in responding to incidents involving artificial satellites and other craft.

### Thunderbird 4

It typically deploys housed within Pod 2 of Thunderbird 2. Among the Thunderbird vehicles, this is International Rescue's specialized submersible designed for underwater operations, such as sea rescues. It is capable of traveling on land for short periods. When launching from Tracy Island, it speeds down the runway—which serves as Thunderbird 2's concealed launch pad—and enters the water directly. Inside, the craft features a watertight hatch for rescue operations requiring diving suits, as well as a compartment to accommodate rescued individuals. It is piloted by Gordon. ### Thunderbird 5

A space station that supports disaster response and rescue operations. John is stationed there permanently, constantly monitoring for accidents and disasters occurring around the world.

### Penelope (FAB-1)

A three-axle, six-wheeled Rolls-Royce owned by Lady Penelope, a former British intelligence agent. She currently serves as an IR agent responsible for intelligence gathering. The vehicle is driven by her butler, Parker—a character who, despite his bumbling nature, often takes surprisingly bold actions. ...sometimes even blowing enemies to smithereens with the missiles and other weaponry mounted on the *Penelope*...

#### Recovery Vehicles (Magnetic Towing Vehicles)

These vehicles launch pylons equipped with electromagnets on either side to latch onto a target vehicle, then use their built-in jet thrusters to perform lifting or towing operations.
They appeared in the second episode when the military quadrupedal tank "Gong" fell into an open-pit mine.


#### The Mole (Jet Mole)

Mounted on a mobile platform, this vehicle burrows underground using a rotating drill, side-mounted tank treads, and rear-mounted jet engines. It is piloted by Scott or Virgil. It appears in episodes 2, 5, and 22.

#### Firefly (Jet Bulldozer)

Used at sites of massive explosions, this vehicle clears away debris to facilitate rescue operations. It destroys large pieces of rubble by firing "nitro-shells" from a launcher located in its nose. Incidentally, "Firefly" is also the name of a famous British tank. It appears in episode 10.
